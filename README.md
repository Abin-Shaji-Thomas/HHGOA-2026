**Official photo frame & VIP pass generator for Hacker House Goa 2026.**

## Live App
→ [https://hhgoa-2026-five.vercel.app/](https://hhgoa-2026-five.vercel.app/)

## Features

- **4 Frame Styles**:
  - **Boarding Pass** (1080×566): Vintage flight ticket aesthetic with tear-off photo stub, seat gate details, barcode, and full multi-socials grid.
  - **VIP Builder ID** (1080×1350): Festival VIP pass badge with lanyard punch hole, hologram seal, and customizable color themes (Emerald & Gold, Sunset Twilight, Arabian Ocean).
  - **Profile Frame** (1080×1080): Circular avatar frame with traditional Goan arch ornament and circular text wrapper.
  - **Team Frame** (1200×630): Squad pass supporting 1 to 4 team members with dynamic auto-centering.
- **2x Ultra-HD Canvas Engine**: Razor-sharp 2x Retina physical resolution exports.
- **Smart Auto-Fit Photo Engine**: Auto-covers portrait, landscape, and square photos + 4-way directional nudge pad & scale zoom controls.
- **Dynamic Multi-Socials Grid**: Displays Full Name, Primary Role, Tech Stack, Location, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, Email, and Portfolio.
- **30+ Unique Builder Classes**: Randomized developer titles (*Async Alchemist*, *Kernel Kadam*, *Merge Conflict Mystic*, *Vite Vibe Coder*, etc.).
- **Selection Radar Hype Score Calculator**: Plug in your X post metrics to calculate candidate radar score.
